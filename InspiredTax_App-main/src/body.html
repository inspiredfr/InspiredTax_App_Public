How provisional tax works (tap to open)

Provisional tax is not a separate tax. It is simply income tax paid in advance, during the year, on income that has little or no PAYE withheld (for example rental, business, freelance, or investment income).

You make up to three payments using an IRP6 form:

  • P1 — by end August (halfway through the tax year)
  • P2 — by end February (last day of the tax year)
  • P3 — optional top-up by end September, to avoid interest
⚠ Filing is separate from paying ▾
Even if you owe nothing extra, you must still submit your IRP6 by each deadline. And your final ITR12 return at year-end is still required — it reconciles what you actually owe against what you have paid.

This page works in three steps: forecast your year, see what to pay at each deadline, then check you are safe from penalties.

Current year movements and closing balances are calculated automatically from amounts captured this year. Opening balances can be entered on this page or the home page of the balance (for example Retirement Deductions, Donations). Only balances that apply are shown as outstanding.

About InspiredTax
InspiredTax
Work out your South African income tax, on your own phone

InspiredTax works out what you owe SARS, or what SARS owes you, and what to pay at each provisional tax deadline, from your own figures. Everything is worked out on this phone, and your tax figures are never sent to us.

What it does
  • Works out your full tax bill on each year's SARS tables
  • Handles salary, investments, rent, your own business, money from overseas, and crypto
  • Works out your travel claim either way — a set rate per kilometre, or what the car actually cost you
  • Medical credits, retirement contributions, and donations to charity
  • Provisional tax dates, what to pay, and whether you are heading for a penalty
  • Shows where your return might raise questions, and what carries over to next year
Privacy & Data

Your tax data is encrypted (AES-GCM), stored on this device and never sent to us. On Android the app contacts Google only to verify your free trial (Google sign-in; one trial-start date kept in the app's hidden Drive folder) and for Google Play purchases. On Android, the app writes an encrypted backup snapshot to its own private storage whenever it locks; if your device has Android's own backup feature turned on, the operating system may sync that encrypted snapshot to your Google account in the background (not controlled by this app). The snapshot is encrypted with a key made from your PIN — a longer PIN makes it much harder to break. You can turn Android's backup off in your device's own Settings > System > Backup. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it erases your data on this phone — export a backup to keep a copy.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is this official SARS software? ▾
No. InspiredTax is an independent tax calculator, estimation and planning tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to SARS or the South African Government. It's not eFiling software — it helps you work out your numbers, assess your tax audit/review risk and extensive tax planning before you file.
What does it calculate? ▾
Full ITR12 computation — brackets, rebates, medical tax credits (s6A/s6B), retirement deductions (s11F) with carry-forward, donations (s18A), travel claims, home office deductions, wear and tear, provisional tax (IRP6), and more. It covers PAYE, employment (IRP5), investment (IT3(b)), rental, business, foreign, retirement and crypto income, and capital gains.
Where is my data stored? ▾
On your device. Your data is encrypted (AES-GCM) and never sent to us. On Android the app contacts Google only for your free trial and for Google Play purchases, and if Android's own backup is turned on, the system may copy an encrypted snapshot of your data to your Google account.
How do I back up my data? ▾
Open the menu and choose Backup, export, Restore. Encrypted backup saves a full copy of your data, locked with your PIN — keep that file somewhere off this device. It is the file you import on a new device. Plain JSON does the same without encryption. The app also keeps an encrypted snapshot on this device each time it locks, and the same screen shows when it was last saved. That snapshot stays on this device, so keep an exported backup as well. You can also turn on Cloud backup on that screen to save an encrypted copy to your own Google Drive. It runs when the app locks, when you sign out, after you finish a step and after 30 minutes of use, and the screen shows when the last one ran and, if one did not, why. Restore from cloud backup on the same screen brings the latest or the previous copy back.
How do I delete my data? ▾
Two options, both under Profile. Reset all data clears all your tax data but keeps your account and PIN, so you can start fresh without signing up again. Delete Account removes everything, including your account itself. Neither can be undone — export a backup first if you might want the data later. If cloud backup has been on, both screens offer to delete your cloud backups from Google Drive as well — tick the box to delete them, or leave it unticked to keep them.
What if a number looks wrong? ▾
Every result traces back to a SARS-cited calculation — tap the (ⓘ) on any figure to see the formula and the section of the Income Tax Act or SARS guide it comes from. If something still looks off, it's worth double-checking your inputs first (a common cause is a field entered under the wrong income/deduction category).
Can I use this for more than one tax year? ▾
Yes. When you're done with a tax year, go to Profile → Tax Year and close it out — the app archives that year's income, deductions, and results as a read-only summary, then automatically carries forward the balances that roll to next year (assessed loss, capital losses, non-deducted retirement contributions, foreign tax credits, donations excess) so you don't have to re-enter them. You can review or override the carried-forward amounts before confirming. Closing out is only offered once next year's tax tables are available in the app.
Does this replace a tax practitioner? ▾
No — all figures are estimates for informational purposes only, not professional financial or tax advice. For anything complex or high-stakes, consult a registered tax practitioner.
