decision logs (2026-07-03 → 2026-10-02). **Read on demand** — before building
in an area, or when blocked on what the owner wants. A decision listed here is
settled: do not re-ask it; build to it or raise a *new* decision with a reason.

- **Columns.** *Status*: **Governs** (a rule future work must follow) ·
  **Built** (in `src/`; recorded so it is not re-litigated) · **Superseded**
  (kept only to show what replaced it) · **Declined** (out of scope until the
  owner reopens it) · **Deferred** (an open item — tracked in `BACKLOG.md`).
- **Sources.** IDs keep their original prefix. Source logs were retired on
  2026-10-04; read them with `git show 15a9d6e:<path>`:
